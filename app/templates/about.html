About the Hertel Lab

Welcome to the Hertel Lab. Our research focuses on understanding the mechanisms that allow for the generation of alternative splicing patterns.

Our research

Our long-term goals are to understand how these processes are regulated, to relate the basic mechanisms of splice-site recognition to biological processes, and to identify strategies to manipulate the expression of splicing isoforms in disease genes.

To achieve these goals, we use a wide variety of approaches, including biochemistry, genetics, deep sequencing, and bioinformatics.

Our team

We are an interdisciplinary team with expertise in molecular biology, computational biology, and physical chemistry. We welcome questions and inquiries about our work.

Visit the Hertel Lab website to learn more about our research and team.
